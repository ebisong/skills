A booking page for a one-person dog grooming shop. Owners book a groom from their phone without an account. Sam, the groomer, runs her day from one screen.
  • An owner can book a groom without creating an account.
  • The owner sees each service's price and length before choosing a time.
  • Only open time slots are offered.
  • The owner gets a confirmation by text message.
  • Sam sees today's bookings in time order.
  • Sam can mark a dog as arrived, in progress, or ready.
  • The owner is texted when the dog is ready for pickup.
    1. Opens the booking link and picks a service.
    2. Picks a time, or sees that the week is full.
    3. Enters her name, phone number and the dog's name.
    4. Sees the booking confirmed.
    5. Gets the confirmation text.
    1. Opens Today and sees the day's dogs in order.
    2. Marks Biscuit ready, which texts Maya.
    3. Checks what the week earned.
